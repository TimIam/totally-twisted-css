About this collection

A little strange. A little nostalgic.

A browser recreation inspired by Berkeley Systems’ Totally Twisted. All 13 named scenes have new illustrations and simplified behavior. Four mathematical fractal scenes are included as an extra collection.

This is an independent recreation, not the original software. The drawings and synthesized sounds are new; exact historical sprites, dialogue, and all rare events are not reproduced.

Open this folder’s index.html in a browser. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari from a hosted copy. Full-screen playback stays inside the browser; it does not replace your lock screen or install a Mac system screensaver.

Controls: Space pauses, N selects the next scene, F starts full-screen playback, and Escape exits. Mime Hunt also supports tapping targets. Sound starts only when you enable it.
